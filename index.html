The case-file method

Read the record.
Test the position.
Show the work.

A document can look persuasive on its own and still conflict with the rest of the file. Our review connects the documents before assessing the argument.

01

Define the question

Confirm the forum, procedural stage, purpose, available record and scope with the instructing team.

02

Build the record

Inventory the documents, reconcile dates and versions, construct the chronology and log what is missing.

03

Test both sides

Trace material propositions to sources. Identify contradictions, adverse facts and the strongest competing explanation.

04

Deliver for review

Separate facts, inferences and open questions. Give counsel a structured assessment and practical revision priorities.

The Precision discipline

A score is a starting point.
The evidence is the substance.

Precision review makes the assessment inspectable, rather than asking you to trust a number.

Where scoring is appropriate, the assessment identifies the applicable rubric, findings and limitations. Different documents and procedural tasks need different review criteria. A score is not a predicted court outcome or a substitute for professional judgment.

Source fidelity

Can the proposition, quotation or date be traced to the supporting material?

Internal consistency

Do the documents, chronology and positions agree, and where do they diverge?

Adversarial testing

What would the other side challenge? Which adverse facts still need an answer?

Completeness and limits

What is missing, unverified or dependent on counsel’s judgment?

Initial inquiry

Start with the question.
Not the whole file.

Tell us the type of review, the general stage and any deadline. Do not send confidential records, names of opposing parties or sensitive case details at this stage.

  1. Fit and scope. We confirm whether the work fits, who will direct it and what is needed.
  2. Agreement and authorization. Applicable participation or engagement terms must be accepted; any required payment must be confirmed before substantive review.
  3. Document instructions. Once cleared, we provide the appropriate submission route and begin the agreed work.

An inquiry does not create an attorney-client relationship or start a review. We are not a law firm and cannot act as your attorney. Case-specific legal analysis is performed at counsel’s direction.
