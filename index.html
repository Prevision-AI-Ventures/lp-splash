Experience

Trial, appellate and complex-dispute work

Our work spans federal and state proceedings, related litigation tracks, commercial agreements and substantial case records. The examples below describe the work and its practical value without identifying the matters.

Appeals, mandamus and rehearing

Record analysis and adversarial testing for appellate review

Work has included appellate and mandamus materials, panel and en banc rehearing analysis, source verification and review of opposing arguments. We examine how the proposed position fits the record, including adverse facts and procedural questions.

Delivered: source-linked findings, draft-review materials, proposed revisions and an adversarial assessment.

Value: the reviewing team can address weaknesses and record discrepancies before relying on the argument.

Complex commercial and financial disputes

Coordinating the record across connected proceedings

Our experience includes large bankruptcy-related records, arbitration and receivership disputes, and analysis of competing positions in complex commercial litigation. We separate the procedural tracks while connecting documents and events that affect more than one issue.

Delivered: integrated chronologies, evidence registers, conflict analysis and preparation materials.

Value: reviewers can see how the proceedings interact and trace material assertions back to the record.

Contested hearings and record review

Preparing the issues, evidence and questions for a hearing

Work has included evidence binders, issue and argument matrices, anticipated-question banks and two-sided rehearsal materials. Record review also examines transcripts and orders to identify what was said, what was decided and what remains unresolved.

Delivered: hearing preparation packs and source-linked written assessments.

Value: the relevant documents and competing positions are organized around the decisions the reviewing team needs to make.

Commercial agreements

Contract findings translated into proposed changes

For a software agreement, we produced a management-level summary and a detailed clause comparison with proposed wording. The review connects individual provisions to the obligations and risks elsewhere in the agreement.

Delivered: a business summary, issue table, side-by-side changes and commercial assessment.

Value: business teams and their advisers can prioritize negotiation points and work from specific proposed language.

Services

What we review and what you receive

Choose a focused document review or a broader case-file engagement. We confirm the purpose, available documents and deliverables before starting.

01

Whole-case review

When the file spans pleadings, orders, transcripts, exhibits and correspondence, reading each item separately can miss the connections. We inventory the supplied record, reconcile dates and versions, build the chronology and test the principal positions against the evidence.

You receive

An organized record, case overview, source-linked chronology, gap register and written assessment. The package identifies supporting material, contradictions and questions requiring further review.

Why engage us

Use it to get an unfamiliar file under control, prepare for a change in stage or give a reviewing attorney a structured starting point.

02

Legal bill review

We examine invoice entries against the engagement terms and available supporting records. Checks can include duplicated entries, calculation discrepancies, inconsistent rates, block billing, vague descriptions and charges that need an explanation.

You receive

A line-item ledger and a prioritized summary that separates visible discrepancies from items requiring time records or clarification. Each question points to the entry and relevant term.

Why engage us

Use it to understand a bill and prepare a focused discussion with the firm, rather than disputing the total without supporting detail.

03

Retainer and engagement review

We examine what the engagement covers, how fees and estimates work, and what happens on conflicts, termination, file access and other operational issues. The review explains how the provisions work together.

You receive

A plain-English issue table, prioritized questions, and side-by-side proposed wording with fallback positions where included in scope.

Why engage us

Use it before signing or renegotiating so the business decisions and points for legal advice are clear.

04

Commercial contract review

We review customer, supplier, SaaS and other business agreements across the main terms and schedules. Scope, payment, liability, indemnities, data use, intellectual property and termination are assessed in context.

You receive

An executive summary, clause-level findings, proposed revisions and a negotiation checklist tailored to the transaction.

Why engage us

Use it to prepare an efficient commercial review, whether you have in-house counsel or engage outside counsel for the legal decisions.

05

Filing and appellate review

We test the argument against the record and the cited authorities, examine adverse facts, and identify gaps in logic, preservation claims or supporting material. The work can cover motions, appellate briefs, mandamus and rehearing materials.

You receive

Source-linked findings, adversarial analysis, revision priorities and proposed improvements within the agreed scope.

Why engage us

Use it as a structured second review that examines the weaknesses as closely as the strengths.

06

Hearing preparation and post-hearing review

Before a hearing, we organize the issues, exhibits, chronology and likely questions. Afterward, we review available transcripts and orders, compare the written record with the matters addressed, and flag inconsistencies or unresolved points.

You receive

A hearing pack or post-hearing review with document references, an issue map and a list of questions and follow-up items.

Why engage us

Use it to prepare with the relevant evidence at hand and to understand the record produced by the hearing.
